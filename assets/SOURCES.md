# Product image provenance

Product and category images were obtained from the public MIT-licensed
[GreenCart repository](https://github.com/diegovilhalva/greencart/tree/main/client/src/assets).
The accompanying license is retained in `PRODUCT-IMAGES-LICENSE.txt`.
Brand names identify the depicted products; Blunder is not presented as an authorised brand partner.
Images are representative; the seller must confirm image rights, exact packaging,
pack sizes, supplier stock and product information before enabling sales.

The launch prices in `data/catalog.json` are the owner's requested promotional
prices, not sourced market prices. No unverified MRP, strike-through price or
80–95% savings claim is published. To display a discount, add a confirmed MRP
(in paise) and a verifiable `priceSource` URL for the exact product/pack.
The discount is calculated from those values, never from an invented percentage.

The original electronics/fashion catalog was replaced at the owner's request.
